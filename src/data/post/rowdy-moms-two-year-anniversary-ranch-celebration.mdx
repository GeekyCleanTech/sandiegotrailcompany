Some afternoons call for a change of scenery. Trade the usual errands for open sky, fresh air, and a crowd of moms who are ready to laugh, mingle, and maybe pull on a pair of boots. On Saturday, November 7, Rowdy Moms is celebrating two years of friendship at Rustic Ranch in Poway, and you're invited.

## Two Years of Rowdy Moms

Rowdy Moms brings local moms together to build friendships, support one another, and enjoy experiences beyond everyday mom life. Its second-anniversary celebration runs from 3 to 6 PM at Rustic Ranch on the Poway Equestrian Center property, with Rustic Ranch Experience as the venue and event-planning partner.

Bring a friend, or come ready to meet a few new ones. Either way, the afternoon is about getting outside, slowing down, and spending a few hours with people who get it.

## An Afternoon at the Ranch

The celebration is built around connection. Local vendors and small businesses will be set up around the property, there will be horse and ranch photo opportunities, and the group will gather on Rustic Ranch's deck for a sound bath with Sacred Sound Haven as the afternoon winds down.

Here's the announced vendor lineup:

- **Rancher Hat Bar**: hat bar
- **Med Lounge**: EMSCULPT giveaway
- **Golden Reserve Celebrations**: cannoli bar
- **Sacred Sound Haven**: sunset sound bath
- **San Diego Trail Company**: optional trail rides
- **The Salty Crown**: mini head massages
- **R3 Recover**: red light and compression
- **HerWay**: B12 shots
- **Chill Vodka**: espresso martinis
- **Mixed Up Cocktails**: canned cocktails
- **Coffee Dose**: matcha
- **Golden Wave Permanent Jewelry**: charm bar
- **The Vibe Check**: aura photography

These are the offerings Rowdy Moms has announced. Admission doesn't automatically cover every product, treatment, or drink, so ask Rowdy Moms when you reserve if you'd like to know what's included.

## Optional Extra: A Short Trail Ride

Curious what the ranch feels like from the saddle? San Diego Trail Company is offering a short, optional trail ride during the celebration. It's an add-on, not a requirement, and the party is the main event whether you ride or not.

## How to Reserve Your Spot

1. Send Rowdy Moms a direct message on Instagram to reserve your spot.
2. Rowdy Moms will reply with payment instructions.
3. Pay the $65 admission directly to Rowdy Moms through Venmo.

Admission is handled entirely by Rowdy Moms. San Diego Trail Company doesn't sell tickets to the celebration.

## Ride Another Day

If an afternoon at the ranch sparks something, come back for a longer ride. San Diego Trail Company offers small-group guided horseback rides through Poway's golden backcountry canyons, a chance to escape the city, connect with nature, and get to know some well-cared-for horses. Regular guided rides are booked separately from the anniversary celebration and don't include event admission.
