website inline. That's why the card image is a video still. To get the moving video into social feeds, upload
the MP4 natively to the post (see captions below) and put the article link in the caption or a Story link sticker.

## 5. Implementation (merged to `main`)

The work reuses the existing blog template (`src/pages/blog/[...slug].astro`), which already carries the brand
tokens from `CustomStyles.astro`: Cormorant Garamond headings, DM Sans body, Deep Saddle Brown #3b2314, Sunbaked
Gold #c49a3c, Canyon Sage #8a9e7b and Warm Linen #f5f0e8.

- **Post schema:** an optional `video` field (`src`, `poster`, `width`, `height`, `label`, `caption`) in
  `src/content.config.ts`.
- **Featured video:** posts with a `video` show it in place of the top image. It autoplays muted and loops with
  `playsinline`, and the controls stay visible so visitors can pause it. Visitors with
  `prefers-reduced-motion` see the poster frame instead of autoplay. The video is re-encoded from 29 MB to 6 MB
  (H.264, faststart, no audio track), which is in line with the site's other clips.
- **Featured photo:** shown in the article body as a figure with descriptive alt text and a caption saying it's
  from a previous gathering. It's also the journal-card thumbnail.
- **Auto-generated meta tags** (`src/utils/postMeta.ts`): every post's title, description, `og:type`, social
  image (in order: frontmatter override, video poster, featured image), article tags and JSON-LD are built from
  the article itself. Frontmatter `metadata` can still override any field. This post overrides only the social
  card image. The `og:video` tags are added through the layout's `head` slot.
- **Share bar on every post** (`src/components/blog/PostShare.astro`), above the featured media and again at the
  end: Facebook, Instagram, Share, email and copy link, each a 44 px touch target, with a polite live region for
  status messages. Each share carries the post's excerpt and its canonical URL, which comes from `site` in
  `config.yaml`. On phones that can share files, the Facebook, Instagram and Share buttons open the system share
  sheet with the featured video file. Because Facebook and Instagram drop pre-filled text, the summary and link
  are also copied for pasting. On desktop, Facebook opens its link sharer, which shows the post's preview card.
  Instagram has no web share link, so it downloads the video and copies the caption.
- **New MDX components:** `PostCta` (a pill button that matches the header's "Book Your Ride") and
  `PostCallout` (a fact box built as a `<dl>`). Everything in them is selectable text, and they stack to one
  column on mobile.
- **Checks:** the site builds with all four posts; `npm run check` passes; the page has one H1;
  and it showed no horizontal scroll at 390 px or 1280 px.
